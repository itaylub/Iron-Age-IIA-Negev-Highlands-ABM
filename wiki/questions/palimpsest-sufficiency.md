# Can autochthonous nomadic processes generate the Iron Age IIA pattern?

## The question (Itay's words, Ch. 5 §5.1)

> “Can autochthonous nomadic processes, operating through temporal mobility and environmental feedbacks, sufficiently generate patterns similar to the observed Iron Age IIA archaeological record?” (Ch. 5 §5.1)

Itay frames the simulation as being of the hypothesis-testing type, "though with a slight nuance": it tests whether the processes of the palimpsest model can generate a record resembling the observed one, "thereby establishing it as a plausible scenario" (Ch. 5 §5.1).

- Ch. 5 §5.6 reports that the ellipse-overlap score improves when simulated activity spreads over more of the region, so longer runs and more households score better while looking implausible. See [experiments](../model/experiments.md).
