# Literature cited by the model documentation

Works that Ch. 5 and App. 5 cite for a model component, grouped by what they support, as author–year exactly as cited. **None is ingested yet.** Full references are in the thesis bibliography (not in this repository). A work gets a `sources/` page only when ingested from the original (`_guide/ingest-sources.md`). Then its line here should link to that page, and the [assumptions register](assumptions.md) status can be checked against what the source actually says, with its scope.

Candidates marked ★ carry a model value or rule directly. They are the ones the ODD will need to cite accurately.

## Herd, subsistence and population

- ★ Seligman et al. 1962: rangeland capacity of 5–10 head of sheep per km² (Ch. 5 §5.2.4)
- ★ Dahl & Hjort 1976: 223 goats per six-person family, about 37 per person (Ch. 5 §5.2.2, §5.2.4)
- ★ Günther et al. 2021: agricultural offset, "who modelled similar systems" (Ch. 5 §5.3.2; App. 5 Table A5.4)
- Hartvigsen 2013; Glassow 1978; Caple & Løvschal 2025: carrying capacity as a concept (Ch. 5 §5.1)

## Things to check
- The 75-year duration is cited to Boaretto et al. 2010 and Finkelstein & Piasetzky 2011 in Ch. 5 §5.2.3, but to Ben-Yosef et al. 2021 and Ben-Yosef 2023 in App. 5 Table A5.4. The Table's "(c. 980–830 BCE)" spans 150 years, while §5.2.3 calls 75 years "the minimum duration attributed to the phenomenon under the low chronology".
